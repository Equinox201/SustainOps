## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This SOP covers measurement of food waste and production planning for buffets
and banquets in all food and beverage outlets.

## 2. Measurement

### 2.1 Weigh and log
Weigh waste at the point it is generated, by category, and log it per service.
Report grams per cover so that results are comparable across outlets.

### 2.2 Categories
Separate preparation waste, spoilage and plate waste. Each category has
different causes and different owners, so they must not be combined in a single
figure.

## 3. Buffet production

### 3.1 Batch replenishment
Replenish buffet lines in small batches on a short cycle instead of filling
large trays at the start of service. Hold the remaining stock in the kitchen
until it is needed.

### 3.2 Forecast from occupancy
Set production quantities from the house count and the share of guests expected
at breakfast, reviewed daily. Adjust for groups and for known low-pickup days.

### 3.3 Pastry lines
For pastry and bakery items, cap the initial display at 60 percent of forecast
and replenish in 15-minute intervals. Record unsold pastry at the end of
service, by item.

## 4. Surplus

### 4.1 Donation
Safe, unserved, properly stored food may be offered to registered food-rescue
partners following the food safety procedure. Plate waste is never donated.

### 4.2 Repurposing
Safe surplus may be repurposed into staff meals or next-day preparations only
within the shelf-life limits set by the food safety plan.

## 5. Targets

### 5.1 Pastry waste
Target pastry waste below 8 percent of quantity produced. Investigate when an
item exceeds 12 percent over a rolling week.
