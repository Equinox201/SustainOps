## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This policy applies to every property and department in the group. It sets the
principles that department-level standards must follow and defines how
sustainability actions are approved and reviewed.

## 2. Principles

### 2.1 Measure before acting
Every sustainability action starts from a measured baseline expressed as an
intensity per occupied room or per cover, so that results stay comparable when
occupancy changes. Absolute totals alone are not accepted as evidence.

### 2.2 Human review
Recommendations produced by any tool, including AI assistants, are decision
support. No action is implemented until a named reviewer has approved it and an
owner and review date are recorded.

### 2.3 No unsupported claims
Teams must not describe an initiative as delivering a saving, or as "green",
"sustainable" or "carbon neutral", unless the claim is backed by recorded data
and the method is documented. Estimates must be labelled as estimates.

### 2.4 Guest privacy
Operational sustainability data must never be linked to identifiable guests.
Guest names, room numbers tied to behaviour, and booking details are out of
scope for sustainability records.

## 3. Group targets

### 3.1 Energy
Reduce electricity use per occupied room by 3 percent per year against the
prior year's baseline, adjusted for occupancy.

### 3.2 Water
Reduce water use per occupied room by 2 percent per year. Laundry and kitchen
use are the priority areas.

### 3.3 Waste
Reduce food waste per cover by 5 percent per year and report landfill diversion
quarterly.

## 4. Governance

### 4.1 Approval
A sustainability action plan needs an owner, a target date, a measurable KPI and
a review date before approval. The approver must not be the author of the plan.

### 4.2 Review cadence
Active plans are reviewed at the date set in the plan, and at least every 90
days. A plan is closed only when its KPI has been reported against baseline.
