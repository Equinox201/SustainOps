## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This SOP governs laundry, linen, towel reuse and guest amenities in guest rooms
and public areas. Version 2 supersedes version 1.

## 3. Towel and linen reuse

### 3.1 Reuse cards
A reuse card is placed in every bathroom at turndown and after each full
service. Cards state the programme plainly and must not make environmental
claims that are not supported by recorded data.

### 3.2 Staff briefing
Room attendants receive a five-minute reuse briefing at the start of each shift
during any programme relaunch, covering card placement, how to treat towels left
on the rack, and what to do when a guest declines.

## 4. Laundry operations

### 4.1 Baseline measurement
Weigh laundry sent to the processing area and record kilograms per occupied
room per day. The baseline is the average of the previous 8 weeks, excluding
weeks with events that distort occupancy.

### 4.2 Escalation threshold
When laundry intensity exceeds baseline by more than 10 percent for two
consecutive weeks, the Executive Housekeeper opens a sustainability case, and
reviews card placement, attendant briefing completion and linen par levels.

### 4.3 Threshold-based laundry batching
Run washers only at or above 85 percent of rated load. During low-occupancy
periods, hold lower-volume categories and batch them into a single scheduled
cycle rather than running partial loads. Record the number of cycles per day.

### 4.4 Wash temperature
Use the lowest wash temperature approved for the linen category and the
chemical programme in use. Changes to programmes require sign-off from
Engineering.

## 5. Amenities

### 5.1 Bulk dispensers
Prefer refillable bulk dispensers over single-use amenity bottles in
bathrooms. Track amenity units issued per occupied room per month.
