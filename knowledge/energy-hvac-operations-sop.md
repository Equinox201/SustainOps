## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This SOP covers air-conditioning, lighting and metering for guest floors and
public areas, and coordination between Engineering and Front Office.

## 2. Occupancy-based operation

### 2.1 Zoning
Guest floors are operated as zones. A zone can be moved to reduced mode only
when all rooms in it are vacant, not blocked for maintenance recovery, and not
pre-assigned.

### 2.2 Low-occupancy floor consolidation
When forecast occupancy is below 45 percent for three or more consecutive
nights, Front Office assigns rooms by floor where guest preferences allow, so
that Engineering can place unused floors in reduced mode. A daily check-in
between Front Office and Engineering confirms eligible floors.

### 2.3 Room-category exceptions
Suites, accessible rooms and rooms with guest-specific requests are excluded
from consolidation unless the guest agrees.

## 3. Set-points

### 3.1 Vacant room setback
Vacant rooms are set back to a maximum of 26 degrees Celsius, and returned to
guest set-point before arrival. Reduced-mode floors use the same limit.

### 3.2 Common areas
Lobby and corridor lighting follows the time-of-day schedule maintained by
Engineering and reviewed each quarter.

### 3.3 Comfort safeguards
Any guest comfort complaint on a consolidated floor is escalated to the duty
engineer immediately and the room is returned to normal operation.

## 4. Metering

### 4.1 Intensity
Report kilowatt-hours per occupied room per night, by floor where
sub-metering exists, and compare to the previous 8-week baseline.
