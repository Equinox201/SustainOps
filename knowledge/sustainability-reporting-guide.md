## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This guide defines how KPIs are named, measured and reported so that
department results can be compared and audited.

## 2. KPI definitions

### 2.1 Intensity metrics
Express KPIs as an amount per occupied room, per cover or per guest night. State
the unit and the denominator in the KPI name, for example "laundry kg per
occupied room per day".

### 2.2 Baselines
A baseline uses at least 8 weeks of data and states the period it covers. When
the baseline is estimated rather than measured, the KPI is labelled as an
estimate.

### 2.3 Targets
A target states the value, the unit and the date, for example "reduce to 1.6 kg
per occupied room per day within 60 days".

## 3. Evidence

### 3.1 Data lineage
Record where each figure came from: meter, scale, log or invoice. Figures
without a source are excluded from reporting.

### 3.2 Estimates
Estimated values are reported separately from measured values and are labelled
as such.

## 4. Cadence

### 4.1 Reporting
Department KPIs are reported monthly; the sustainability manager consolidates
quarterly. Progress is recorded against the baseline, not against the previous
period.
