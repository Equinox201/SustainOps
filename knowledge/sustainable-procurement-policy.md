## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Scope
This policy applies to recurring operational purchases above a defined spend
threshold, including food, amenities, linen, chemicals and packaging.

## 2. Supplier screening

### 2.1 Criteria
Suppliers of high-impact categories are screened against environmental
management practice, packaging, transport distance, labour standards and
documentation quality. Screening is recorded on a one-page scorecard.

### 2.2 Scorecard thresholds
A supplier scoring below 60 percent is not approved for new orders. A supplier
scoring 60 to 75 percent is approved conditionally with an improvement plan and
a six-month review.

## 3. Packaging

### 3.1 Reduce single-use
Prefer concentrate, bulk and returnable packaging. New product listings that
add single-use plastic need a written justification from the requesting
department.

## 4. Evidence

### 4.1 Recertification
Supplier documentation is refreshed annually. Claims made by suppliers must be
supported by certificates or test reports before they are repeated in guest or
marketing material.
